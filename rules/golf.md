# Golf: Rules of Golf (excerpts)

Paraphrased from the Rules of Golf (R&A and USGA) for the demo case. The wording is close to the official text
but not verbatim, and the IDs are this project's own. Before relying on a result, replace each excerpt with the
official wording from https://www.randa.org/rog or https://www.usga.org/rules. Keep the `## ID Title` headings:
the council reads them, and cases cite clauses by ID.

## GOLF-KVC Definition: known or virtually certain
Source: Definitions, "Known or Virtually Certain"
The standard for deciding what happened to a ball, for example what caused it to move. Known or virtually
certain means more than just possible or probable. It means either that there is conclusive evidence that the
event happened, or that, although there is a small degree of doubt, all reasonably available information shows
it is at least 95% likely that the event happened.

## GOLF-PG Definition: when a ball is on the putting green
Source: Definitions, "Putting Green"
A ball is on the putting green when any part of the ball touches the putting green. The closely mown fringe
around the green is part of the general area, not the putting green.

## GOLF-9.2b Deciding what caused a ball to move
Source: Rule 9.2b
When a player's ball at rest has moved, there are four possible causes: natural forces such as wind or water,
the player, the opponent (in match play), or an outside influence. The ball is treated as moved by the player
only if that is known or virtually certain. If it is not known or virtually certain that the player, opponent or
an outside influence caused it to move, it is treated as having been moved by natural forces.

## GOLF-9.3 Ball moved by natural forces
Source: Rule 9.3
If natural forces such as wind or water cause a player's ball at rest to move, there is no penalty, and the ball
must be played from its new spot. (Exception: a ball on the putting green that had already been lifted and
replaced must be replaced; see Rule 13.1d.)

## GOLF-9.4b Player lifts, touches or causes the ball to move
Source: Rule 9.4b
If the player lifts or deliberately touches their ball at rest, or causes it to move, the player gets one penalty
stroke, and the ball must be replaced on its original spot. Exceptions with no penalty include accidentally
causing the ball to move while it is on the putting green (Rule 13.1d) and accidental movement while searching
for it.

## GOLF-9.4 Penalty for playing from a wrong place under Rule 9.4
Source: Rule 9.4, penalty statement
If the ball had to be replaced under Rule 9.4 but the player instead plays it from where it came to rest, the
player has played from a wrong place and gets the general penalty under Rule 14.7a (two penalty strokes in stroke
play). A player who both caused the ball to move and played it from a wrong place gets only the general penalty,
not an extra stroke.

## GOLF-13.1d Ball moved on the putting green
Source: Rule 13.1d
There is no penalty if the player accidentally causes their ball to move while it is on the putting green; the
ball must be replaced. This exception applies only to a ball on the putting green.

## GOLF-14.7a Playing from a wrong place
Source: Rule 14.7a
A player must make a stroke at their ball from the right place. A player who plays from a wrong place gets the
general penalty: two penalty strokes in stroke play.

## GOLF-20.2c Video evidence and the "naked eye" standard
Source: Rule 20.2c
When the Committee decides questions of fact, the use of video evidence is limited by the "naked eye" standard:
if facts shown by video could not reasonably have been seen with the naked eye, that video evidence is
disregarded even if it indicates a breach of the Rules.
