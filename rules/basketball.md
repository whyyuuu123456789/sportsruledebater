# Basketball: NBA playing rules (excerpts)

Plain-language summaries of NBA rules for the demo case, NOT the official wording, and the IDs are this
project's own. Before relying on a result, replace each excerpt with the official text from the NBA Official
Rules (https://official.nba.com) and the NBA replay rules. Keep the `## ID Title` headings: the council reads
them, and cases cite clauses by ID.

## NBA-TIME.1 Scoring with less than 0.3 seconds ("Trent Tucker rule")
Source: NBA Official Rules, timing regulations (the 0.3-second rule)
A player needs at least 0.3 seconds on the game clock (or shot clock) to secure possession of the ball and
attempt a field goal. When play resumes with 0.2 or 0.1 seconds on the clock, a player cannot catch the ball and
then shoot: a field goal can be scored only by tapping or tipping the ball directly, for example a tip-in or an
alley-oop touch of an inbound pass.

## NBA-TIME.2 Shot at the end of a period
Source: NBA Official Rules, timing regulations
A field goal attempted at the end of a period counts only if the ball has left the shooter's hand before time
expires (the game clock reaches 0.0).

## NBA-REPLAY Instant replay at the end of a period
Source: NBA instant replay rules
Officials may use instant replay to decide whether a made field goal at the end of a period was released before
time expired and, when 0.2 seconds or less remained, whether it was scored legally by a tap or tip. The call on
the floor is changed only when the replay provides clear and conclusive visual evidence that it was wrong.
